## Introduction

As part of working in the AV integration world, I sometimes need to visit sites that are still under construction or have
active electrical installations. A requirement for working on these sites in the UK is to have an ECS card,
which demonstrates competence in health, safety and environmental awareness.

I'm studying for the [Electrotechnical Certification Scheme (ECS)](https://www.ecscard.org.uk/) Health, Safety and Environmental Awareness Assessment,
so I built this quiz to help me revise. It uses every question from the official revision guide. Hopefully it will be useful for others too.

The real assessment has 50 multiple choice questions drawn from the question bank, with 30 minutes to answer them. You
need 43 correct to pass. The answers in the assessment can appear in a different order from the guide, so the quiz
shuffles them each time.
